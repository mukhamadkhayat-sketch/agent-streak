How it works

  1. Connect your wallet. Use the wallet that holds your Agent ID (MetaMask, Binance Wallet or Trust Wallet). No QR login, no pairing codes, no account creation.
  2. Check in once per UTC day. One transaction, gas only. Your streak grows by one each consecutive day.
  3. Earn soulbound badges. At 3, 7, 14 and 30 days the matching badge mints straight to your wallet — a permanent, non-transferable proof of consistency.

FAQ

What counts as "a day"?

A UTC day: block.timestamp / 86400. The day flips at 00:00 UTC, which is 07:00 WIB. Check in any time before the flip to keep your streak alive.

What happens if I miss a day?

Your current streak resets to 1 on your next check-in. Your best streak is never lost, and badges you already earned stay with you forever.

Are the badges worth anything?

No. Badges are soulbound reputation markers: they cannot be transferred, sold, burned or redeemed. They carry no monetary value, no yield and no promise of return — now or in the future.

Who can check in?

Only addresses holding an Agent ID (SPACE ID Agent ID registry on BNB Chain). The contract checks balanceOf(address) > 0 on-chain before accepting a check-in.

What does it cost?

Check-in itself is free (value 0). You only pay BNB Chain gas — roughly 59,000 gas for a regular check-in, about Rp 32 at 0.05 gwei. A tier-up check-in (badge mint) costs a bit more.

What can the contract owner do?

Only pause/unpause check-ins in an emergency, and transfer or renounce ownership. The owner cannot mint badges, change tiers, edit streaks, touch user data or move funds — the contract never holds funds. The badge contract has no owner at all.

Is my wallet safe here?

The site only ever asks your wallet to sign the checkIn() transaction, which you review and confirm yourself. It never requests token or NFT approvals, never asks for your seed phrase or private key, and never uses QR-code or pairing-code login.
